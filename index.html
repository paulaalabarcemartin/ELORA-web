Our story

A more conscious kind of beauty

ELORA was born from a simple wish: to bring the calm of a Mediterranean morning into everyday self-care.

Salt air, olive trees, wild lavender and the first light of the sun on the water — those were the things that made us feel good, and we wanted our skincare to feel the same. Every formula starts with plants, flowers and minerals and is made in small batches, without ingredients we would not use ourselves.

We believe beauty is not about perfection but about care: taking a moment for yourself, knowing what you put on your skin and where it comes from. And because no skin glows on a tired planet, we choose glass, aluminium and recycled materials, and we never test on animals.

Our values

Five promises behind every bottle, jar and tube.

01

Vegan beauty

No animal-derived ingredients — not even beeswax or lanolin. Only the richness of plants.

02
